## Can wheelchair users see Longteng Bridge?

The principal remains can be viewed from the entrance and gentler areas. However, this is not an entirely level, barrier-free park. Some angles involve slopes, stairs or uneven ground. Actual access depends on current conditions.

## Which paths are best for a stroller?

Stay near the main entrance and open viewpoints rather than forcing a stroller into steps or narrow stretches. With two adults, one can stay with the child while the other briefly visits another viewpoint, then reunite at an agreed place.

## How should slower-moving visitors get out of the car?

Choose a safe drop-off place away from bends and moving traffic. Let passengers wait while the driver parks legally, then meet again. Stopping permissions, fees and pedestrian routes must be checked against current signs.

## Where can visitors rest or use the toilet?

Consult recent Google Maps place information before departure and follow on-site signs. Do not treat an unverified online toilet pin as a permanent facility. Plan a longer rest at Shengxing Old Street or a subsequent indoor attraction rather than relying entirely on facilities at the monument.

## Is the rail bike accessible to people with reduced mobility?

The operator sets health and mobility requirements. Read the conditions or contact the operator before paying. Photographs alone are not sufficient to judge boarding difficulty.

## Low-walking half-day suggestion

**Longteng Bridge entrance viewpoint → lunch on Shengxing Old Street → short stop at Shengxing Station.** This includes Sanyi's highlights without requiring long walks at every stop. In heavy rain, shorten Shengxing or switch to an indoor woodcarving attraction.
