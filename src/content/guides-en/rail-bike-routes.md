## Who should choose Route A?

Route A approaches the Old Mountain Line from Shengxing Station, crosses Yutengping Iron Bridge, offers a distant view of the northern broken bridge and reaches the southern remains. If the south bridge is essential to your visit, A is usually the clearest starting point.

It suits visitors who want to understand how the northern and southern ruins relate and are happy to start their itinerary near Shengxing.

## Who should choose Route B?

Route B links Longteng Station, Tunnel No. 2 and Shengxing Station. The focus is Shengxing, an old-street stop and a shorter railway experience. This route does not pass Yutengping Iron Bridge.

Choose it if eating or strolling at Shengxing matters more than reaching the southern ruins.

## Who should choose Route C?

Route C starts at Longteng Station and travels across Yutengping Iron Bridge toward the tunnel group. It emphasizes bridge structures, tunnels and mountain railway engineering.

It suits visitors who enjoy valley scenery and engineering and want to connect the ride with the nearby bridge viewpoint.

## Which route is best with children or older visitors?

Scenery is only one factor. Check age and height requirements, comfort with heights and tunnels, and how much walking is involved at stops. If someone cannot ride, the group may split between the rail bike and a nearby walk, but agree on the meeting point beforehand.

## Where can I check fares and departure times?

Prices, departure times, ticket collection deadlines and extra services can change. Use the [official Old Mountain Line Rail Bike information](https://www.oml-railbike.com/_pages/info/index.php). We provide decision guidance rather than reproducing timetables that may expire.

## Still undecided?

- “I specifically want the south broken bridge.” → A.
- “I want Shengxing Old Street and lunch.” → B.
- “I enjoy iron bridges and tunnels.” → C.
- “I want all three.” → Consider separate rides or prioritize the experience that cannot be substituted.
