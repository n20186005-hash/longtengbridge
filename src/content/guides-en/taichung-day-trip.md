## Planning a Taichung day trip

Travel from Taichung to Sanyi and combine Longteng Bridge with Shengxing Station in one day to avoid needless backtracking.

## Decide how to travel

Drivers can visit the bridge in the morning and move to Shengxing for lunch and a walk. Public transport travelers should decide the order using available transfers from Sanyi Station.

## Keep booked activities central

If you have rail bike tickets, plan around the check-in time and adjust meals and monument viewing, especially during crowded periods.

## Frequently asked questions

**How many stops should I plan?** For a first visit, the bridge, Shengxing and one additional activity are usually more comfortable than an overpacked day.

**Does the bridge require a ticket?** General outdoor viewing and the paid rail bike are separate.

**Where should I navigate?** Use [Longteng Bridge](https://maps.app.goo.gl/55jN8AsmsDdr5xGs5) for the ruins and the ticket's station for rail bike check-in.

## From city to railway hills

Within a day, you can discover Japanese-era railway engineering, a wooden station and Hakka settlement culture. The itinerary is most rewarding as an introduction to the Old Mountain Line, not just a collection of photo stops.

## From morning to afternoon

Study Longteng's brick arches and earthquake history in the morning, rest at Shengxing Old Street around lunch, then see the station building and old-line facilities in the afternoon. Move these stops around a fixed rail bike departure if needed.

## Photos and the journey home

Morning light may help show the bridge's depth; afternoon offers details of the timber station. Before returning to Taichung, check mountain traffic or connecting trains and leave extra time on holidays.
