## Where should you park if you only want to see the ruins?

Search Google Maps for Longteng Bridge and look for legal parking near the main visitor area. Mountain space is limited, and parking may change because of events, construction or traffic management. Do not assume an informal open lot is a permanent public car park.

On arrival, check signs for parking permission, fees and separate areas for coaches and cars.

## Where should rail-bike passengers park?

Start with the collection or check-in location on your booking. Routes A, B and C can involve different starting points. Having Longteng Bridge in the itinerary does not mean every passenger should navigate to the same car park.

> The simplest rule: follow the ticket's check-in station if you have a reservation. Use the main monument area only if your purpose is to see the ruins without a ride.

## How much extra time should you allow on weekends?

Avoid relying on a fixed number of minutes. Holidays, flowering seasons, events and afternoon traffic can all lengthen parking and walking time. Build a generous buffer if a rail-bike check-in follows your visit.

## Is roadside parking allowed?

An unmarked space or a spot shown in someone else's photo is not necessarily a legal parking area. Mountain roads are narrow and careless parking can obstruct passing vehicles, residents or emergency access.

Follow signs, road markings and staff directions. If no legal place is available, use a designated car park and walk from there.

## What works for families and older visitors?

Where safe, let slower-moving passengers get out at a suitable drop-off point before the driver parks. Avoid bends, steep roadway sections and areas where traffic converges.

Visitors with strollers or wheelchairs can prioritize viewpoints near the entrance rather than climbing stairs for a particular photo. See also the [family guide](/en/family-guide/) and [accessibility advice](/en/accessibility/).

## Parking checklist

- Am I visiting only the ruins, or must I report to a ticket station?
- Does Google Maps show the correct destination?
- Do signs clearly allow parking, and are there fees?
- Will my vehicle obstruct traffic or residents?
- Have I left enough time before the next check-in?
