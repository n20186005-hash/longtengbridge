## How to plan from Miaoli City

Traveling within the same county still involves hilly roads, so keep transfer time separate from the time you intend to spend sightseeing.

## Before setting out

For half a day, visit Longteng Bridge and Shengxing Station. With a full day, consider adding Sanyi woodcarving or the Old Mountain Line Rail Bike.

## Choose your priorities

Rail bike rides require booking and a check-in buffer. Without tickets, enjoy the brick arches, railway history and nearby walks without a fixed departure time.

## Frequently asked questions

**Should I cancel if it rains?** A short visit may work in light rain; heavy rain or road restrictions call for a change of plans.

**Do I need a bridge ticket?** Viewing the ruins outdoors is separate from the paid rail bike.

**Where should I navigate?** Use [Longteng Bridge](https://maps.app.goo.gl/55jN8AsmsDdr5xGs5) for the ruins or your booking's station for the rail bike.

## One county, different terrain

The journey from Miaoli's urban center to Sanyi involves hill roads. Longteng Bridge's brick arches demonstrate why the Old Mountain Line needed a valley crossing, while Shengxing Station offers a view of mountain railway station culture.

## Half-day or full-day choice

**Half day:** View the bridge first, then walk around Shengxing Station. **Full day:** Add either a woodcarving stop or a rail bike ride—not necessarily both—so travel and check-in do not consume all your rest time.

## Adjusting for your group

Older visitors may prefer easily accessible viewpoints rather than every section of the ruins. Children can look for three materials—red brick, a steel bridge and the timber station building—with enough time set aside for lunch.
