## Why pair Longteng Bridge with Shengxing Station?

Both are part of the Old Mountain Line railway story. The experience moves naturally from bridge engineering to a historic wooden station building. Shengxing Station also connects with the old street and some rail-bike itineraries, making it the simplest next stop for first-time visitors.

## What should you do on Shengxing Old Street?

Use the old street for lunch, snacks and a relaxed break. It can become crowded on weekends. If you have a timed booking later in the day, allow more time for meals rather than scheduling everything tightly.

## How should you explore Sanyi's woodcarving culture?

Choose a museum for a concise cultural introduction, or visit the Shuimei area to browse shops and the woodcarving district. This is a different theme from railway history but an important part of Sanyi's identity.

## Is Huoyanshan a convenient extra stop?

Huoyanshan is a hike requiring attention to fitness, weather and trail conditions. It is not an easy five-minute stop for a typical family tour. Give hiking at least half a day and treat Longteng Bridge as a shorter visit before or after it.

## Other Old Mountain Line sights

- **Yutengping Iron Bridge:** understand the replacement railway alignment after the earthquake.
- **Old Mountain Line tunnels:** best explored according to the rail-bike operator's current routes.
- **Old Tai'an Station:** continue the theme of historic railway stations.
- **Liyutan Reservoir surroundings:** a choice for landscapes and engineering scenery.

## Choose according to your group

| Travellers | Suggested combination |
| --- | --- |
| Families with young children | Bridge + Shengxing + one indoor stop |
| Older visitors | Bridge entrance viewpoint + old street lunch + station |
| Railway enthusiasts | Bridge + iron-bridge route + Shengxing or Old Tai'an Station |
| Photographers | Early bridge views + Shengxing station building + mountain railway scenery |

## The most useful way to cut stops

If time is short, remove the most distant, physically demanding or least relevant place first. Exploring three well-connected places at an unhurried pace is usually more memorable than racing through six landmarks.
