## Should you eat lunch right beside the bridge?

For a short bridge visit, move the full meal to Shengxing Old Street or central Sanyi. Choices and opening conditions near the viewpoint can change, so do not make one restaurant the only possible solution.

## What food can you find on Shengxing Old Street?

Look for Hakka dishes, noodles, traditional rice preparations and snacks. The old street combines lunch and strolling. On busy weekends decide in advance between a proper seated meal and sampling several snacks instead of making everyone choose after arrival.

## How should families and older visitors choose a restaurant?

Look beyond popularity. Consider indoor seating for a rest, accessible toilets, slopes and steps from parking, dishes suitable for young children or older adults, and expected waiting times.

## How should meals fit around the rail bike?

Work from your reporting time. If departing near Shengxing, lunch can fit before or after the ride. If departing from Longteng Station, allow time for driving and parking between the two places rather than squeezing food into a short pre-check-in gap.

## How do you filter places on Google Maps?

Search for "Shengxing Old Street food" or "Sanyi Hakka cuisine". Read recent opening information, parking descriptions and photographs. Hours can change unexpectedly; contact the restaurant before an important group meal.
