## How should you plan a half-day visit to Longteng Bridge?

If you have only half a day, do not feel obliged to book the Old Mountain Line Rail Bike simply to add an activity. The bridge ruins and Shengxing Old Street are enough for a trip with a clear theme.

## What to prepare before arriving

Begin at the main viewing area for the broken bridge. Read the interpretation panels, study the brick piers and allow enough time for photographs. Afterwards, travel to Shengxing Station and its adjoining old street for a break.

## What to see and in what order

You can return after lunch or a snack. To explore Sanyi's woodcarving shops as well, budget for the additional drive and parking rather than rushing between sites.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is half a day enough?** Yes, for the bridge and Shengxing Station at a comfortable pace. A timed rail-bike reservation changes the schedule and needs its own buffer.

**Do I need a ticket to see Longteng Bridge?** Viewing the outdoor historical remains is different from taking the paid Old Mountain Line Rail Bike. Check the operator's current rules if you want to ride.

**Where should I navigate?** For the main ruins, use [Longteng Bridge on Google Maps](https://maps.app.goo.gl/55jN8AsmsDdr5xGs5). For the rail bike, follow the reporting station printed on your booking.

## The cultural story behind this half-day trip

Longteng Bridge and Shengxing Station connect two parts of the Old Mountain Line's history: bridge engineering and railway station life. The route suits travellers with limited time and does not require a ticket at every stop.

## Suggested visiting order

Start at the bridge's principal viewpoint. Read the panels and compare the red-brick arches with the later iron bridge in the background. Continue to Shengxing Station for its wooden station building, then eat or have tea on the old street. Choose your transport according to current road and transit conditions.

## If you need to shorten the day

If weather worsens or parking takes longer than planned, keep only the bridge and Shengxing Station. Do not assume that the road between them is a short, flat walk: mountain roads and weekend traffic can change the journey considerably.
