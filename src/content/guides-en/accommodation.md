## When is an overnight stay worthwhile?

If you want at least two substantial activities—such as rail biking, woodcarving, a longer hike or a more distant railway heritage stop—two days and one night will be more comfortable than trying to fit everything into one day.

## Why stay in central Sanyi?

Meals and transportation are generally easier to arrange, with access to Sanyi railway station and major roads. It suits travellers who want practical accommodation and plan to continue toward other parts of Miaoli or Taichung.

## Who should stay around Shengxing?

Visitors seeking a quieter mountain-town atmosphere, a relaxed old-street stroll or another Old Mountain Line morning. Check mountain road conditions, nighttime lighting, parking and how far you must carry luggage.

## What should families check before booking?

- Suitable beds and fall-prevention arrangements.
- Stairs in rooms and shared spaces.
- Slopes or steps between parking and the room.
- Breakfast and nearby meal availability.
- Cancellation terms in case the weather changes.

## How should you search Google Maps?

Start with "Sanyi accommodation" or "Shengxing accommodation" and review the locations. Search recent reviews for parking, noise, hot water and children. Confirm essential requirements with the property directly rather than relying on a listing label.

## How can you split two days?

**Day 1:** Sanyi woodcarving district → Shengxing Old Street → overnight stay.

**Day 2:** Longteng Bridge → rail-bike ride → return journey.

Swap the days if your booked departure is on the first afternoon. The main rule is to group mountain drives and check-in stations efficiently.
