## How should motorcyclists plan a visit?

Curves, roadside visibility, lawful parking and afternoon weather are the main considerations on Sanyi's mountain roads.

## Before you ride

Inspect tires, carry rain protection and charge your phone. Do not pull over abruptly for a photo when you glimpse the bridge. Ride to a safe, legal parking area first.

## On arrival

Watch for pedestrians, coaches and vehicles turning near the viewpoint. Reduce speed on busy days, and keep helmets and belongings away from the carriageway while photographing.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can motorcycles stop directly beside the bridge?** Follow signs and road markings; a small vehicle does not justify blocking a passage.

**Do you need a ticket for the ruins?** The outdoor monument and paid rail-bike service are separate. Verify the operator's rules for a ride.

**Where should you navigate?** Use [Longteng Bridge on Google Maps](https://maps.app.goo.gl/55jN8AsmsDdr5xGs5) for the main ruins. If riding the rail bike, navigate to the station shown on your booking.

## Riding conditions on the Old Mountain Line roads

The hills around Sanyi have curves, gradients and tourist traffic. Keep sight distances in mind and expect temporarily stopped vehicles. Wet leaves and puddles may remain after rain, so slow down before reaching the visitor area.

## Walking after parking

Park legally before taking out your camera; do not sort luggage on a roadside shoulder. Follow the safe pedestrian route to the north viewpoint. Look first at the overall outline of the red-brick arches, then examine the piers.

## Photography with a motorcycle itinerary

Treat Shengxing Station and the bridge as separate park-and-walk stops. Do not leave helmets or gloves on barriers where they could fall, and never block a pedestrian route with your bike.
