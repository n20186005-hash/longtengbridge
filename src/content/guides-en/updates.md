## Which sources do we prioritize?

- Historic names and heritage status: [National Cultural Heritage Database](https://nchdb.boch.gov.tw/assets/overview/monument/20031125000001).
- Local sightseeing information: [Miaoli tourism website](https://miaolitravel.net/article.aspx?sNo=04004516).
- National tourism information: [Taiwan Tourism Administration](https://www.taiwan.net.tw/m1.aspx?id=c100_342&sno=0001110).
- Rail bike routes, tickets and passenger rules: [Old Mountain Line Rail Bike](https://www.oml-railbike.com/_pages/info/index.php).
- Navigation and current road assessments: Google Maps.

## What should not be treated as permanently fixed?

Ticket prices, sessions, added departures, refunds, parking fees, parking-area access, shuttles, road restrictions and special-weather operations may change. Our guides describe how to choose; actual transactions and departure decisions should rely on the organization publishing the relevant information.

## Why don't we claim a site is “open now”?

Unless a page connects to trustworthy live data, a real-time claim can mislead. Access to an outdoor monument does not guarantee that surrounding roads, paths or rail bike services operate normally in all conditions.

## How should Google Maps be used?

It is useful for navigation, roads and recent place information, but user-added places, reviews and photos are not official announcements. Use on-site signs for parking, toilets and temporary walking routes.

## How do Google ratings and review counts get updated?

Displayed figures originate in publicly available Google Maps data, not ratings created by this website. The figures last checked on **September 13, 2026** were **4.3 stars and 24,443 reviews**, with the site categorized as a historic monument. New reviews can change these counts. Once verified, we update the text and identify the source on our [Sources page](/sources/). Third-party Google ratings are not presented as this site's own ratings in structured data.

## What if you notice an error?

Use the site information in the footer to locate sources and check whether a difference results from an update, temporary announcement or naming confusion. A future version may provide a formal correction contact; this site currently does not collect user accounts or run a user registration system.
