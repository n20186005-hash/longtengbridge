## How to use this pre-trip checklist

First-time visitors to the Sanyi hills most often miss the distinction between parking locations, rail bike boarding stations and return-trip timing.

## What to check before leaving

Confirm your exact Google Maps destination, parking arrangements, weather forecast, transport home and phone charge. Wear non-slip walking shoes and carry water.

## Plan the order of your visit

Keep saved tickets and check-in instructions handy if you have a reservation. Without a booked activity, leave the itinerary flexible.

## Frequently asked questions

**What if an area is closed?** Respect notices and use an open viewpoint; never bypass a barrier.

**Do I need an admission ticket?** Outdoor heritage viewing and the paid Old Mountain Line Rail Bike are separate activities.

**Which map destination is right?** Navigate to [Longteng Bridge](https://maps.app.goo.gl/55jN8AsmsDdr5xGs5) for the ruins; use the station printed on your rail bike booking for that activity.

## Decide your primary destination first

If you are visiting the historic bridge, navigate directly to the ruins. If you hold a rail bike ticket, navigate to the departure station on the order. Those may require different parking arrangements; similar place names do not mean the same entrance.

## Check the day before

Review forecasts, roads, operator notices, tickets, a power bank and drinking water. For older adults or small children, consider slopes and walking distances; public transport travelers should arrange the return leg in advance.

## Three rules on site

View the monument only from open areas, park legally and follow signs, and adapt immediately to thunderstorms or closures. No photograph is worth crossing a railing or standing in active traffic.
