## Taking the train to Longteng Bridge

Longteng Bridge is not located next to a station currently served by Taiwan Railways. After riding to Sanyi, arrange the final leg.

## What to check beforehand

Look up train services to Sanyi Station and available buses, shuttles or taxis for that day. Do not confuse the old tourist destination at Shengxing Station with a present-day passenger railway station.

## Return travel and check-in

Plan the train home and any taxi pickup in advance; waiting for transport in the hills differs from city conditions. If booking a rail bike, add its separate check-in time.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can I walk from Sanyi Station?** Treating a lengthy walk along mountain roads as the default is not recommended, particularly with children or luggage.

**Is the monument ticketed?** Outdoor heritage viewing is separate from the paid rail bike.

**Where should I navigate?** [Longteng Bridge on Google Maps](https://maps.app.goo.gl/55jN8AsmsDdr5xGs5) is the monument. Use the booking's station for a rail bike ride.

## Why an old station is not a transfer point

Shengxing was once part of the Old Mountain Line and now serves a different function from active Sanyi Station. Railway passengers must first arrive at a working station and arrange travel into the hills.

## Transfers and backup options

After reaching Sanyi, check whether a bus serves the stop you need and its timetable. If departures are unsuitable, arrange a licensed taxi for both directions ahead of time instead of arriving at the monument without a return option.

## Walking and photography safety

You can observe the historic structure closely from open viewpoints, but the rural roads between Sanyi Station and the ruins do not provide a continuous pedestrian path. Do not walk long distances on narrow, winding shoulders simply to save a fare.
