## How to spend two days and one night in Sanyi

Separate historic sites, railways, local craftsmanship and food into two days, giving yourself enough time at each place.

## Planning before departure

Spend the first day at Longteng Bridge, Shengxing Station and Old Street. The second can feature a woodcarving museum, Shuimei's craft streets or attractions appropriate to your group's mobility.

## A flexible order

Choose lodging for transport convenience, not solely by proximity to one attraction. For a rail bike trip, secure the ticket and session first, then organize the rest of the visit.

## Frequently asked questions

**Must I stay overnight in Sanyi itself?** No. Compare places to stay based on your transport and second-day destination.

**Is the bridge ticketed?** The open-air monument differs from the paid Old Mountain Line Rail Bike; check its operator if booking.

**Which map destination is appropriate?** Use [Longteng Bridge](https://maps.app.goo.gl/55jN8AsmsDdr5xGs5) for the ruins, or your ticket's station for a rail bike ride.

## A two-day story from railways to woodcarving

On day one, discover the Old Mountain Line's bridges, stations and surrounding settlements. On day two, explore woodcarving museums and the Shuimei craft district to connect railway infrastructure with Sanyi's local industries.

## Suggested day-by-day sequence

**Day one:** Visit the bridge in the morning and Shengxing Station and Old Street in the afternoon. Adjust around any rail bike reservation. **Day two:** Explore woodcarving exhibitions, craft streets and a leisurely meal; avoid cramming in distant stops both days.

## Accommodation and rainy-day options

Pick lodging based on whether you drive or need train connections. In rain, shift indoor exhibitions to the main part of the day and shorten outdoor heritage stops according to conditions.
