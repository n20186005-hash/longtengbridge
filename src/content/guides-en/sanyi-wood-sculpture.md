## Combining the museum and Shuimei district

Sanyi is not only about the historic railway. Its woodcarving industry is central to local crafts and the area's identity.

## What to check before you go

For a systematic introduction to woodcarving history, consult Sanyi Wood Sculpture Museum exhibition and opening notices. For a streetscape experience, explore the Shuimei commercial area and its craft shops.

## On-site visit order

The museum makes a useful rainy-day alternative, but closure days and admission prices can change. Respect photography restrictions and do not touch objects not designated for interaction.

## Frequently asked questions

**How does it pair with Longteng Bridge?** See the bridge in the morning and woodcarving in the afternoon for a day of engineering and craftsmanship.

## How woodcarving became a Sanyi specialty

Local woodworking developed into a distinctive craft industry with a museum and the Shuimei district. Where railway bridges emphasize engineering, woodcarving showcases materials, hand techniques and local industrial change.

## Museum first, neighborhood second

Check permanent and temporary museum exhibitions, then visit Shuimei's carving shops. At the museum, study grain, carving marks, three-dimensional forms and artistic styles; on the street, compare works from different studios.

## Transport and photography

The museum and Longteng Bridge have separate entrances and require separate map destinations. Some exhibitions may prohibit flash or all photography; follow signs.
