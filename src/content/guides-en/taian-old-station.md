## Planning a two-station heritage excursion

Tai'an Old Station adds another perspective on how the Old Mountain Line shaped buildings, settlements and rail operations.

## Before traveling

Consider Longteng's bridge structure, Shengxing's wooden station and Tai'an's station complex within one railway-history theme. Together, they show different stages of the mountain line.

## Moving between places

The sites do not form a continuous pedestrian route. Driving or arranging a legal transfer makes timing easier. Never enter closed railway facilities.

## Frequently asked questions

**Must I see all three in one day?** Railway enthusiasts may enjoy that itinerary; most first-time visitors can prioritize Longteng and Shengxing.

**Is the bridge ticketed?** Outdoor viewing differs from the paid Old Mountain Line Rail Bike.

**Which destination should I enter?** This page is about **Tai'an Old Station** in Houli, Taichung. Verify the dedicated map result's name and district. Enter Longteng Bridge separately if visiting it.

## Tai'an Old Station in railway history

Tai'an Old Station, in Taichung's Houli District, is part of Old Mountain Line station heritage. Its architecture differs from Shengxing's timber character, reflecting different periods in building and railway development.

## Observing the station

From the station forecourt, study the facade's geometric lines, entrance, windows and name sign. Then view platform and track remains from open areas and read the historical interpretation on site.

## Crossing county lines

Longteng Bridge is in Sanyi, Miaoli, whereas Tai'an Old Station is in Houli, Taichung. You need transport between them; this is not a short walk inside one attraction. Never cross closed tracks or unsafe facilities for photographs.
