## Planning a dog walk at Longteng Bridge

Start with open, level viewing areas. Avoid surfaces heated by direct sun and places where pedestrians and vehicles mix.

## What to prepare

Bring a leash, drinking water and waste bags. Do not allow a dog past safety barriers, near unstable slopes or in pursuit of wild animals.

## Which other venues allow pets?

Pet access at the Old Mountain Line Rail Bike, museums and indoor establishments is decided separately. Do not assume that permissions for an outdoor monument extend to nearby attractions.

## Frequently asked questions

**What time is best in summer?** Avoid the strongest midday sun and check both ground temperature and your pet's comfort.

**Is an admission ticket required?** Walking around the outdoor ruins differs from booking a paid rail bike ride.

**Where should I navigate?** [Longteng Bridge on Google Maps](https://maps.app.goo.gl/55jN8AsmsDdr5xGs5) is the monument; follow the ticket's station address for rail bike trips.

## Pet etiquette at a heritage site

The brick bridge sits among walking routes, vegetation and wildlife habitat. Leashing and cleaning up protect both the historic environment and other visitors.

## A safe walking sequence

Identify open pedestrian areas first and avoid steep slopes, stairs and traffic crossings. Bring enough drinking water in summer and check the pavement; after rain, watch for mud and slippery surfaces.

## Rules elsewhere in Sanyi

Shengxing Old Street businesses, museums and the rail bike can each set pet policies. Ask before entering, and keep pets out of restricted areas and away from wildlife and dangerous slopes.
