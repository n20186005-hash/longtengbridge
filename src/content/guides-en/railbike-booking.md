## How to plan a rail bike booking

The rail bike and free outdoor viewing at Longteng Bridge are different choices. Confirm what experience you want before purchasing.

## Before you arrive

Consult the operator's current routes, departures, prices and check-in stations. Keep your booking and review the late-arrival, rescheduling and refund policies.

## Check the weather and service

Look again at operating notices and forecasts one day before travel. Mountain services may change in severe weather.

## Frequently asked questions

**What if I do not receive an order confirmation?** Check the original booking channel and contact its customer service before making a second payment.

**Must I book to see the bridge?** No rail bike ticket is needed for general viewing of open heritage areas; the ride is a separate paid activity.

**Where should I navigate?** This guide points to **Old Mountain Line Rail Bike — Longteng Station**. Confirm the search result's name and county; a fixed Google Place ID is not yet available here. Add the heritage ruins as a separate map stop if visiting both.

## Different management roles

Conservation of the Yutengping Bridge heritage asset and ticketing for the tourist rail bike are distinct responsibilities. Direct reservation, price and date-change questions to the actual seller or operator.

## Booking to departure, step by step

Choose a date, check route and passenger eligibility, select a departure, pay and save the booking. Reconfirm the station, check-in time and weather the previous day; allow time to park and use the restroom.

## Cancellations and service changes

For weather or operational changes, follow the original booking channel's official policy, not informal social-media comments. If a ride is canceled, free viewing of the bridge and a walk around Shengxing Old Street may be alternatives.
