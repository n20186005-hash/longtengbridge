## Choosing the right parking destination

The Longteng Rail Bike Station and the main historic bridge viewing area are not interchangeable. If you have a timed reservation, plan around it first.

## Before arriving

Check the station, booked route and check-in time on your order. Allow separate time for ticket collection and waiting; parking at the historic monument does not mean you have reached the boarding point.

## On-site movement

Whether a car needs to be moved after visiting the bridge depends on the open pedestrian routes and current parking rules. Save two distinct map locations before departure.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can I walk directly between the parking areas?** Do not assume there is a direct link. Follow station and on-site walking directions.

**Is a ticket required for the ruins?** Outdoor heritage viewing differs from the paid rail bike service.

**Which map result should I use?** This page focuses on **Old Mountain Line Rail Bike — Longteng Station**. Verify the search result's station name and county; no fixed Place ID has been established. Use a separate navigation stop if you also plan to see the bridge ruins.

## Why the destinations differ

Yutengping Broken Bridge is a historic railway ruin; the current rail bike is a tourism use of the former Old Mountain Line. The heritage viewpoint, ticket/check-in points and starting stations for individual routes are not the same thing. Cross-check similarly named search results against your reservation.

## The simplest sequence

**With a reservation:** Visit the assigned check-in station first and complete ticket checks; fit the bridge viewpoint around available time. **Without a reservation:** See the historic bridge first and consider Shengxing afterward. Allow time for mountain driving, parking again and walking between stops.

## Interpreting parking photographs

Open ground shown in online photographs may have been temporary parking or part of an event. Use official station directions, pavement markings and current signs—not an old image—to decide if parking is permitted.
