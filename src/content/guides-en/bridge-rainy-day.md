## Planning a rainy-day visit

Mountain rain may affect paths, visibility for photographs and rail bike outings. Check up-to-date forecasts before departure.

## Before you travel

In light rain, shorten your time outdoors, wear shoes with good grip and protect camera equipment. Thunderstorms, downpours, typhoons and road restrictions are reasons to postpone the trip.

## On-site priorities and alternatives

Consider indoor woodcarving-related venues around Sanyi, but verify opening arrangements separately. Do not take cover underneath the damaged bridge, beside a stream or beneath steep slopes.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can I walk the paths immediately after rain stops?** Surfaces may remain slippery; decide based on actual access conditions and on-site restrictions.

**Is a ticket required?** Outdoor viewing and the ticketed Old Mountain Line Rail Bike are separate experiences. Check the operator if you have a ride planned.

**What destination should I enter?** Use [Longteng Bridge](https://maps.app.goo.gl/55jN8AsmsDdr5xGs5) for the ruins and the station listed on your ticket for the rail bike.

## Rain and hillside terrain

Paths and sloping ground around Sanyi's heritage site may be slippery after rainfall. Mist can make the red-brick arches atmospheric, but lightning and intense rain also increase risk.

## A shorter wet-weather itinerary

For light rain only, view the bridge from an easily reached public area and then consider Shengxing Station or an indoor woodcarving cultural space, subject to opening hours. Never push past a closure caused by heavy rain, rockfall or blocked roads.

## Photographing the bridge in mist

Rain and mist can emphasize layers of arches in the landscape. Protect and dry your lens, and take photographs only from safe points—not streambeds, beneath the bridge or closed slopes.
